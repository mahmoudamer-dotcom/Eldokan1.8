# ElDokan Admin v1.6.18

Enhanced per-seller reporting only. Seller Dashboard is unchanged.

- Seller selector + date range
- Financial split: net sales, platform commission, seller net
- Ratings: customer product ratings, manual admin rating, automatic cancellation rating
- Cancellation count/rate, lost value, reason extraction and reason summary
- Product inventory/performance: statuses, stock, sold/never sold, units, revenue, commission, seller net, cancellations/refunds
- Top categories and monthly revenue trend
- Internal performance score
- Excel-compatible export with all report sections
